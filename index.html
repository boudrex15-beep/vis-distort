How to calibrate
  1. If calibrating one eye, cover the other eye.
  2. Sit at your normal reading distance and keep it.
  3. Stare at the center dot the whole time.
  4. Drag the grid points until the lines look straight to you.
  5. Click Save profile when the grid looks straight.

Tip: click a point, then use arrow keys to nudge it (Shift+arrows for fine steps).

About Vis‑Distort

This tool is for people with metamorphopsia — distorted (wavy) vision, commonly from macular degeneration. It pre-warps what is on screen with the opposite of your distortion, so that it can look straighter to you.

Two steps

  1. Calibrate: stare at the center dot and drag the grid until the lines look straight to you. Save it as a profile (one per eye if they differ).
  2. View: open an image, PDF, or video. It is shown through your correction. Press space to compare with/without.

Important to know

  • The correction is anchored to your fixation point. It works best when you look at the center dot and keep the same viewing distance you calibrated at.
  • Everything runs on your computer. Your files and calibration never leave this device.
  • This is an assistive aid, not a medical treatment. If your distortion changes noticeably or suddenly, contact your eye doctor — sudden change can signal wet AMD, which is urgent.
