Vis‑Distort

A viewer for low vision from macular degeneration, which usually mixes fuzziness (blur) and darker spots. Open an image, a PDF, or a video — or paste text to read — and make it easier to see:

  • Magnify — the biggest single help, especially for fuzziness. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
  • Contrast, brightness & colour modes — make things stand out and help dark spots.
  • Scrolling text — easier to read with central vision loss; you can hold your gaze slightly to the side and let the words come to you. Choose white-on-black or black-on-white text.

Everything runs on your computer — your files and text never leave this device.

Honest limits

  • Magnification and contrast improve clarity, but no software can restore detail your retina no longer senses, or refill a true blind spot.
  • This is an assistive aid, not treatment. If your vision changes suddenly, contact your eye doctor — that can be urgent.
