How to calibrate
  1. If calibrating one eye, cover the other eye.
  2. Sit at your normal reading distance and keep it.
  3. Stare at the center dot the whole time.
  4. Drag the grid points until the lines look straight to you.
  5. Click Save profile when the grid looks straight.

Tip: click a point, then use arrow keys to nudge it (Shift+arrows for fine steps).

Vis‑Distort Aids

This is an expanded viewer for age-related macular degeneration, which often mixes three problems: distortion (wavy lines), fuzziness (blur), and darker spots. Different tools help each.

What helps what

  • Magnify — the biggest single help, especially for fuzziness.
  • Contrast, brightness & colour modes — make text stand out and help dark spots.
  • Scrolling text — easier to read with central vision loss; you can hold your gaze slightly to the side of the words.
  • Distortion correction — the calibrated warp, straightening bent lines. Press space to compare.

All of these stack together, and everything runs on your computer — nothing leaves this device.

Honest limits

  • Magnification and contrast improve clarity, but no software can restore detail your retina no longer senses, or refill a true blind spot.
  • The distortion correction works best when you look at the center dot and keep the viewing distance you calibrated at.
  • This is an assistive aid, not treatment. If your vision changes suddenly, contact your eye doctor — that can be urgent.
